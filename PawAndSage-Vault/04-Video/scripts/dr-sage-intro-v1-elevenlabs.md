# ElevenLabs-ready script

I'm Dr. Sage. <break time="0.4s" /> I'm an AI avatar created by Paw and Sage, powered by veterinary science.

<break time="0.7s" />

Think of me as your personal feline translator — turning confusing habits into opportunities to play, bond, and enrich your cat's life.

<break time="0.8s" />

The handsome guy next to me is Paw. <break time="0.4s" /> He's my co-worker... and my constant shadow.

<break time="0.8s" />

We know you're always asking the internet: <break time="0.3s" /> "Why does my cat do this? Is something wrong?" <break time="0.5s" /> And clear, science-backed answers are hard to find.

<break time="0.7s" />

So — we're starting a series to take one weird or funny thing your cat does... and figure out the WHY behind it.

<break time="0.5s" />

Our goal is to help you understand their wild instincts and make your indoor life together way more fun.

<break time="0.9s" />

Follow Paw and Sage so you don't miss a video, and drop your most confusing cat behavior in the comments. <break time="0.4s" /> We might decode it NEXT.

# Notes

- Total break time ≈ 6.0s. For ~45–55s of read, that's right at the upper bound — if delivery feels choppy, drop the 0.7s breaks to 0.5s first.
- Em-dashes do light pacing work where breaks would be overkill (after "translator", "So").
- Ellipses on "co-worker..." and "does..." create a setup/payoff beat without tag overhead.
- Capitalized "WHY" and "NEXT" are the only emphasis cues — sparse on purpose so they land.
- If using Eleven v3 audio tags, good candidates: `[warmly]` before "The handsome guy", `[playful]` before "We might decode it NEXT."
