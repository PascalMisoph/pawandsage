**Key takeaways**

- **PrettyLitter** is amorphous silica-gel crystals. The company says pores absorb urine, trap odor, and let moisture evaporate, and that you scoop solids rather than scooping urine clumps.
- **Color key, in PrettyLitter's words:** dark yellow / olive green = typical; blue = alkalinity; orange = acidity; red = visible blood. PrettyLitter says it cannot diagnose or prevent disease.
- **Tidy Cats Free and Clean** is unscented clumping clay with activated charcoal, no fragrances or dyes, a low-dust claim, and directions to fill 3–4 inches and remove solids daily. Purina also claims 10-day odor control when used as directed.

- **Where Tidy Cats wins:** scoopable clumps, a familiar clay texture, store-aisle availability, and a better match for most automatic boxes, including current Litter-Robot models.

## What PrettyLitter's Colors Actually Mean

PrettyLitter's official how-it-works page is the source to use, not a screenshot from social media.

The company says the litter is **amorphous silica gel**, a porous crystal that absorbs urine and traps odor-causing bacteria, then lets moisture evaporate so the crystals feel dry again. Solids are scooped; urine is not supposed to form a clay-style clump.

On color, PrettyLitter says the crystals react to acidity, alkalinity and visible blood:

- **Dark yellow / olive green:** typical range, in the company's wording.
- **Blue:** may indicate a more alkaline pH, which PrettyLitter links to a higher struvite-stone risk or urinary infection patterns.
- **Orange:** may indicate a more acidic pH, which it links to issues such as metabolic or kidney tubular acidosis and a higher oxalate-stone risk.
- **Red:** visible blood, which it says may be associated with lower urinary tract disease, stones, inflammation, some kidney disease, clotting disorders, or, more rarely, cancer or injury.

The same company, on its help page, says PrettyLitter **is not diagnostic**, cannot detect every problem, and cannot replace a veterinarian. That is the line we will keep. If the color is not the typical green and it stays that way, or the cat strains, hides, or stops eating, call the vet. Do not wait on a subscription bag to "confirm" anything.

## What Tidy Cats Free and Clean Is For

[Tidy Cats Free and Clean Unscented](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D2FYM5T3?tag=pawandsage21-20) is a conventional **clumping clay**. The listing describes an unscented formula made without fragrances or dyes, with activated charcoal, a low-dust easy-pour bag, and tight clumps. Purina's directions are simple: pour 3–4 inches, remove solids daily, top up to keep that depth, and wash the box with warm water and mild detergent when you change the litter.

Purina also claims "guaranteed 10-day odor control when used as directed." That is a brand promise tied to following the directions, not a measurement we made. If odor is your only issue, daily scooping and enough boxes usually matter more than switching brands every month.

Tidy Cats has other formulas, including scented Instant Action and the [Designed for Litter-Robot](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DVZD2LJR?tag=pawandsage21-20) clay. For this comparison we used the unscented Free and Clean bag, which is the fairer match to unscented PrettyLitter. If you need Litter-Robot compatibility, use the official Whisker list rather than assuming every Tidy Cats bag sifts the same way.

## Automatic Boxes, Switching and Cost

Crystal litter and clumping clay behave differently in a machine. Whisker says PrettyLitter can work in Litter-Robot 3 and is **not recommended** in Litter-Robot 4, 5/Pro or EVO because translucent or white crystals can confuse sensors. If you already own a newer Litter-Robot, this PrettyLitter vs Tidy Cats question is already answered: stay with a listed clay, or pick a different box. [Best litter for Litter-Robot](/blog/best-litter-for-litter-robot) has the table.

Switching in a regular box should still be gradual. A new texture, especially crystals after clay, is a common reason a cat starts going beside the box. Keep the same box, mix or run a second box of the new litter, and give the cat time.

We are not publishing a monthly cost comparison. PrettyLitter is often sold as a subscription or a 6 lb bag the listing describes as up to a one-month supply; Tidy Cats Free and Clean is a 16 lb (or larger) clay bag you can buy once. Check the current price for the size you will actually use, including shipping.
