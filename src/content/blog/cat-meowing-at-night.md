**Quick answer:** VCA Animal Hospitals lists **attention-seeking, sexual behavior (heat), play, medical problems, discomfort and aggression** as the most common reasons cats vocalize. For **middle-aged and older cats**, VCA recommends a **vet exam** to rule out pain, endocrine disease and hypertension. For healthy cats, the fix is a busier day and **not rewarding** the meowing.

## Rule out medical causes first

- **Older cats:** VCA says older cats with declining senses or cognitive function may wake more at night and vocalize more. Cornell Feline Health Center lists loud vocalizing, often in the middle of the night, among signs of cognitive dysfunction in cats 10 and older.
- **Hyperthyroidism and hypertension:** Cornell says night-time vocalizing is relatively common in cats with these conditions, and that hypertension can cause vision loss, leading to anxiety and confusion.
- **Pain or discomfort:** VCA includes these among the common causes.

## Not spayed or neutered?

VCA notes some vocalization is linked to estrus and mating in adult cats that are not neutered. Talk to your vet about spaying or neutering.

## The plan for a healthy cat

1. **Fill the day.** VCA says a stimulating daytime routine with regular offerings of food, social play and exploratory toys may help shift your cat's schedule so it sleeps through the night.
2. **Play before bed.** AAHA says cats are most active at dawn and dusk, and a play session before bed can help tire your cat out; see [how much playtime a cat needs](/blog/how-much-playtime-does-a-cat-need).
3. **Feed on a schedule.** If your cat yells for breakfast at 4 AM, a timed feeder means food arrives on schedule rather than when you get up. See [Petlibro vs PetSafe automatic feeders](/blog/petlibro-vs-petsafe-automatic-feeder).
4. **Don't reward it.** VCA says never reward vocalization with food, attention or play, because it reinforces the behavior. Reward quiet instead. Expect it to get louder briefly before it stops (VCA calls this an "extinction burst").
5. **Never use physical punishment** (VCA).
