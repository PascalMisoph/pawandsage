Summer grooming is not about making your cat look tidy. It is about comfort.

Loose coat, mats, sharp claws, litter dust, and travel messes all become more annoying in warm weather. A small routine prevents most of it before it turns into a fight.

## 1. Brush Before the Coat Mats

Short-haired cats may only need a few quick passes. Long-haired cats need more frequent checks around the armpits, chest, belly, and rear legs, where mats build quietly.

Brush in short sessions. Stop while your cat is still relaxed.

For shedding specifics, read [why cats shed so much in summer](/blog/cat-shedding-summer).

## 2. Build a Grooming Station

Keep the brush, towel, treats, and cleanup option in one predictable place. The routine becomes easier when you are not hunting for supplies while your cat is still in the mood.

**What helps:** The [HICC PET Glove Wipes](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BRML7424?tag=pawandsage21-20) make sense for small summer messes because they are glove-shaped and individually wrapped. Use them for quick paw, coat, face, or rear-area cleanup when your cat tolerates it. Do not use wipes to treat irritation, wounds, or skin disease.

## 3. Check Nails Every Two Weeks

Summer scratching, climbing, and grooming can make long claws more noticeable. You do not need to trim every time you check. Just look for sharp tips, snagging, or claws that are getting too curved.

## 4. Watch Skin, Not Just Fur

Normal shedding leaves healthy skin underneath. Call your vet if you see bald patches, redness, scabs, fleas, dandruff that suddenly worsens, excessive licking, or pain when touched.

Grooming is a chance to notice changes early. It is not a replacement for diagnosis.

## 5. Support the Coat From the Inside

Hydration and diet matter. Wet food, clean water stations, and a cooler room all support normal summer comfort. If your cat is a reluctant drinker, start with [how to get your cat to drink more water](/blog/get-cat-to-drink-more-water).

## The 10-Minute Weekly Checklist

- Brush loose coat.
- Check mat-prone areas.
- Wipe small messes only if needed.
- Check nail tips.
- Check skin.
- Refresh water stations.

Do it before the hottest part of the day, reward generously, and keep the routine boring. Boring grooming is successful grooming.
