**Key takeaways**

- **Check for illness first.** New peeing outside the box can be a urinary, kidney, thyroid or diabetes problem, per Cornell. Call the vet before you buy.
- **High peeing is not spraying.** Cornell says spraying cats stand, quiver the tail and mark vertical surfaces in several places. A tall box will not fix marking.
- **Wall height matters most.** Look for 8 inches or more on the sides your cat backs into.
- **Open-top with tall walls** gives containment without the cramped feel of a hood.
- **Size up.** A cramped box pushes the cat to the edge.

## Is my cat peeing high, spraying, or having a medical problem?

Before spending money, sort out which problem you have.

- **High peeing:** the cat is in the box, in a normal pee posture or standing, and urine goes over the rim or down the outside wall. This is a box problem, and taller walls usually fix it.
- **Spraying:** Cornell describes cats standing, lifting and quivering the tail, and leaving small amounts of urine on vertical surfaces in several consistent places. That is marking, not a box size issue. Cornell notes that 10% of neutered males and 5% of neutered females still spray.
- **Medical:** Cornell lists urinary tract inflammation, kidney disease, thyroid disease, diabetes and mobility or cognitive problems as causes of litter box trouble. If peeing outside the box is new, go to the vet first.

## How should I set up a litter box for a cat that pees high?

- **Put the tall side where the cat backs up.** Watch where urine lands now and turn the box so the highest wall faces that way.
- **Keep the same litter at first.** Change one thing at a time. If you also want to change litter, see [tofu vs clay cat litter](/blog/tofu-vs-clay-cat-litter).
- **Fill deeper.** A shallow layer encourages cats to stand on the edge to avoid wet spots.
- **Scoop daily.** Cats avoid dirty patches and edge toward the rim.
- **One box per cat plus one,** in separate, quiet places, is the common rule for multi-cat homes. For automatic options, see [best automatic litter box for multiple cats](/blog/best-automatic-litter-box-multiple-cats).
- **Clean old urine with an enzyme cleaner** so the cat is not drawn back to the same spot.
