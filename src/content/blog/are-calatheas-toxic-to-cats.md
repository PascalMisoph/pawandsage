**Quick answer:** No. The ASPCA lists *Calathea* species as **non-toxic to cats, dogs and horses**, and its prayer plant entry (*Calathea insignis*, also called peacock plant, rattlesnake plant and zebra plant) says the same.

## What the ASPCA plant database says

- **Scientific name:** *Calathea spp.*
- **Family:** Marantaceae
- **Other common names:** prayer plant, peacock plant, rattlesnake plant, zebra plant
- **ASPCA verdict:** Non-toxic to cats

The verdict above comes straight from the [ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center's entry](https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/aspca-poison-control/toxic-and-non-toxic-plants/calathea). ASPCA Poison Control is the reference most US veterinarians use for plant questions.

## A safe tropical swap

If you love big patterned leaves but have a cat, calathea is one of the best swaps on the ASPCA list for toxic tropicals like [monstera](/blog/is-monstera-toxic-to-cats), [pothos](/blog/is-pothos-toxic-to-cats) and [peace lily](/blog/are-peace-lilies-toxic-to-cats), which all contain irritating calcium oxalates.

## Non-toxic is not chew-proof

Cats still chew calathea leaves, and a cat that eats a lot of any plant can vomit. Watch for plant-chewing that becomes a habit; it can be a boredom signal, and [indoor enrichment](/guides/indoor-cat-enrichment) usually helps more than deterrents.

## Watch the plant care products

Calatheas are fussy about water and humidity, so they often come with leaf shine sprays, fertilizer and pest treatments. Those are separate from the plant verdict. Keep them stored away and ask your vet before using any product on a plant your cat touches.

## When to call the vet anyway

Non-toxic means the plant is not expected to poison your cat, not that it is food. Call your veterinarian if your cat vomits repeatedly, seems lethargic, stops eating, or if you are not sure the plant really was calathea (look-alikes and nicknames cause a lot of confusion). If you think your cat ate something toxic, call the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center at [(888) 426-4435](tel:+18884264435) (a consultation fee may apply).
