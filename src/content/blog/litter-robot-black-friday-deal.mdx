## How to Judge a Real Litter-Robot Discount

Use the same five checks we use on our [Black Friday cat deals](/blog/black-friday-cat-deals) hub:

1. **Price history beats list price.** Tools like camelcamelcamel and Keepa show what people actually paid recently. A real deal is meaningfully lower than that history—not just lower than an inflated strikethrough.
2. **Read the bundle title.** "Robot Only," "Supply Bundle," and "Protection Bundle" are different carts. A lower headline price that removes liners, a fence, or warranty extras may not be cheaper overall.
3. **Count consumables.** Drawer liners, carbon filters or OdorTrap packs, and litter add up. A $50 robot discount can disappear in a few months of proprietary add-ons if you were not planning to buy them.
4. **Confirm the return window.** For the 2026 holiday season, Amazon notes that many eligible items bought between November 1 and December 31, 2026 can be returned into late January 2027. Confirm on the specific listing. That window matters because cats need time to accept a new box.
5. **Be honest about cat temperament.** A discounted robot your cat refuses is not a deal. Introduce it beside the old box with auto mode off at first.

## A Practical November Shopping Plan

1. **Now (early November):** Decide LR4 vs EVO vs open-top vs budget rake using the comparison pages above.
2. **Add the ASIN to a price tracker** and note what you would happily pay.
3. **Through November 20–30:** Check Amazon and Whisker a few times per week—not only on Friday morning.
4. **Before checkout:** Confirm bundle contents, litter compatibility, floor requirements, and return dates.
5. **After delivery:** Run a slow introduction. Keep the old box available. Automatic mode only after the cat is comfortable.

Bringing home a young kitten? Automatic modes have minimum weights. Start with a regular box from the [new kitten checklist](/blog/new-kitten-checklist) until your kitten is heavy enough.
