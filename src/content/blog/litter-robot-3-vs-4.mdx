**Quick answer:** The **Litter-Robot 3 is discontinued.** Whisker's own LR3 Connect page says so and listed it as out of stock on November 18, 2026. If you are buying new, the real choice is the **[Litter-Robot 4](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B7835C46?tag=pawandsage21-20)** (up to 4 cats, 3–25 lb, sold on Amazon) or the compact **[Litter-Robot EVO](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0G6GDJVYB?tag=pawandsage21-20)** (up to 2 cats, 3–19 lb). A used LR3 only makes sense at a low price and with the warranty and safety trade-offs below. Already own an LR3 that works? Keep it running; you do not need to upgrade.

**Key takeaways**

- **LR3 status:** discontinued by Whisker; no new units. Warranty still runs from the original ship date (1 year, or 3 with extended).
- **LR4 adds:** SafeCat with OmniSense laser and weight detection, live waste and litter levels, QuietSift, a bigger drawer and a wider opening.
- **Entry:** 10.25 × 15.25 in on the LR3 vs 15.75 × 15.75 in on the LR4.
- **Cat weight:** LR3 needs at least 5 lb with no upper limit; LR4 is rated 3–25 lb.
- **Same litter rules:** clumping clay, per Whisker.

## What Whisker Says Changed

On the LR3 Connect FAQ, Whisker lists the core features both models share (patented sifting, odor management, multi-cat design, cat safety features, modular parts) and then what the LR4 adds: the optional OdorTrap system, the SafeCat system with OmniSense detection for safety and real-time tracking of waste and litter levels, a QuietSift cleaning cycle, an enlarged waste drawer and a wider opening.

The biggest practical upgrades are the **opening** (much wider on the LR4, useful for big or hesitant cats) and **sensing**. The LR3 detects a cat by weight; the LR4 adds laser curtain sensors that also measure drawer and litter levels, so the app can tell you exactly how full things are.

## Large Cats: Where the LR3 Still Has a Point

The LR3 has no upper weight limit, and Whisker gives a globe interior of 15 to 17 inches high (depending on litter level) by 20 × 20 inches. The LR4 is rated to 25 lb. For a cat well over 25 lb, Whisker's current answer is the Litter-Robot 5 (rated to 30 lb), not a used LR3. Other large-cat options are in our [Litter-Robot alternatives](/blog/litter-robot-alternatives) guide.

## Small Cats and Kittens

The LR3 needs at least 5 lb to trigger its cat sensor. Whisker says lighter kittens can use it in semi-automatic mode (unit off, cycle run manually). The LR4 and EVO are rated from 3 lb.

## Should You Buy a Used Litter-Robot 3?

Before you pay for one, check:

1. **Ship date.** Warranty is counted from the original ship date, so most used LR3s are out of coverage.
2. **Globe, seal and drawer condition.** Cracks and smells are hard to fix.
3. **Model.** Connect versions need 2.4 GHz WiFi and cannot join shared networks such as dorms or apartment-complex WiFi, per Whisker.
4. **Price vs a new EVO or LR4.** Compare with current pricing in our [Litter-Robot Black Friday deal](/blog/litter-robot-black-friday-deal) guide before you decide.
5. **Cleaning.** Deep clean any used unit before your cat uses it. Our [how to clean a Litter-Robot 4](/blog/how-to-clean-litter-robot-4) guide covers the globe and base rules, most of which apply to older globes too; follow your LR3 manual for model-specific steps.

## Already Own an LR3? Keep It Running

- **Use the right litter.** Whisker says the Litter-Robot works with almost any clumping litter but not with non-clumping, newspaper or wood-based litters. Our [best litter for Litter-Robot](/blog/best-litter-for-litter-robot) list applies to the LR3 too.
- **Liners.** Whisker's [Litter-Robot Waste Drawer Liners](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F3QTK9ZT?tag=pawandsage21-20) are listed as compatible with all models; on the LR3 you push the rolled edges into the four drawer tabs.
- **Upgrade when it breaks,** not before. The LR4 is the most direct replacement; the [EVO](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0G6GDJVYB?tag=pawandsage21-20) is Whisker's suggested option for a similar experience in a smaller footprint.
