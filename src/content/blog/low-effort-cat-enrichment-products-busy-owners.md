Busy owners do not need guilt-based cat shopping. They need products that reduce friction and keep working after the first week.

Low-effort enrichment is not "ignore your cat." It is setting up the home so your cat has things to watch, solve, climb, drink from, and revisit without you inventing a new activity every day.

## 3. Food Work You Can Repeat

The best busy-owner enrichment turns part of a meal into work. Treat balls, lick mats, beginner puzzles, and short dispenser games all help if you rotate them.

## 4. A Window Setup

A window perch or safe viewing spot is one of the lowest-maintenance upgrades because the outside world supplies the novelty. Birds, leaves, people, rain, and light changes keep the view from becoming static.

## What Not To Buy

Skip products that:

- Require you to supervise constantly when you wanted independent enrichment.
- Are too loud for your cat.
- Add clutter but no route, food work, or watching value.
- Promise to fix boredom without changing routine.

## The Busy-Owner Formula

Use one passive layer, one food-work layer, and one human-led play window:

- Passive: window, shelf, perch.
- Food work: puzzle, treat hunt, dispenser.
- Human: one short play session before a meal.

That is enough to make most indoor days richer without turning your life into a cat activity calendar.
