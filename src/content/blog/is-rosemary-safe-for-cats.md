**Quick answer:** Yes, the plant. The ASPCA lists rosemary (*Rosmarinus officinalis*) as **non-toxic to cats, dogs and horses**. Concentrated **rosemary essential oil** is a separate question: Texas A&M's veterinary college lists rosemary among essential oils toxic to pets.

## What the ASPCA plant database says

- **Scientific name:** *Rosmarinus officinalis*
- **Family:** Lamiaceae
- **Other common names:** anthos
- **ASPCA verdict:** Non-toxic to cats

The verdict above comes straight from the [ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center's entry](https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/aspca-poison-control/toxic-and-non-toxic-plants/rosemary). ASPCA Poison Control is the reference most US veterinarians use for plant questions.

## Is it safe for cats to smell rosemary?

A rosemary plant in the room is fine by the ASPCA's verdict. Concentrated oil is different. ASPCA Poison Control's advice on diffusers is to use them only for short periods in an area your cat cannot access, and to avoid them altogether if your cat has breathing problems. The Merck Veterinary Manual recommends keeping pets out of the room while a diffuser runs and ventilating afterward.

## Rosemary in cat food?

You may see "rosemary extract" on pet food labels. That is an ingredient decision made by the food's manufacturer, not the same as a cat eating the plant or being exposed to concentrated oil. If you have questions about a specific food, ask your veterinarian.

## Herb garden neighbours

[Basil](/blog/is-basil-safe-for-cats) is also non-toxic on the ASPCA list. [Mint](/blog/is-peppermint-oil-safe-for-cats) and [lavender](/blog/is-lavender-safe-for-cats) are listed as toxic to cats.

## When to call the vet anyway

Non-toxic means the plant is not expected to poison your cat, not that it is food. Call your veterinarian if your cat vomits repeatedly, seems lethargic, stops eating, or if you are not sure the plant really was rosemary (look-alikes and nicknames cause a lot of confusion). If you think your cat ate something toxic, call the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center at [(888) 426-4435](tel:+18884264435) (a consultation fee may apply).
