**Quick answer:** Choose the **[Litter-Robot 4](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B7835C46?tag=pawandsage21-20)** if you want a sealed globe, proven multi-cat tracking by weight, and no paid video plan for core features. Choose the **[PETLIBRO Luma](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0G599SG3B?tag=pawandsage21-20)** if you prefer an open-top box with a camera, active fan odor control, and you already have hard flooring. If $600 feels like too much to start, try a cheaper rake-style box first.

Both boxes scoop after visits so you empty a sealed waste drawer instead of daily scooping. The real differences are design (enclosed globe vs open top), floor rules, litter rules, and how much of the "smart" experience is free versus subscription. ## Open-Top vs Globe: The Decision That Matters Most

**Litter-Robot 4** hides waste inside a sealed globe and drawer. That helps with odor and with cats who dig enthusiastically, and it keeps curious toddlers or dogs out of the litter more easily. Some cats hesitate at an enclosed entrance at first.

**Luma** is open on top. Skittish cats and large cats who dislike tunnels often accept open boxes faster. You can also see the litter bed at a glance. The trade-off is less visual privacy and a different odor strategy: Luma uses a built-in fan through a carbon deodorizer rather than a fully enclosed globe.

If your cat already refuses covered boxes, start with Luma's open design or a simple open manual box before buying any robot. If odor containment is your top stress point in a small apartment, the enclosed LR4 is usually the safer bet on paper.

## Floor, Litter and Placement Rules

These constraints knock more buyers out than the feature lists suggest.

**Flooring.** PETLIBRO is explicit: place Luma on a hard, level surface only, with at least 4 inches of space around it. Carpet can throw off the weight sensor and the safety system. Litter-Robot 4 also needs accurate weight sensing for automatic mode; Amazon's listing notes the scale is most accurate on a firm surface, and Whisker's comparison chart points to a Carpet Tray add-on when you use carpet.

**Litter.** Whisker recommends standard-weight clumping clay for Litter-Robot models. Plant-based, non-clumping, newspaper, and wood-pellet litters typically do not sift correctly. PETLIBRO says Luma works with clumping, tofu, and mixed litters, and ships filters for different grain sizes; do not use non-clumping crystal or pine, or ultra-fine grass-seed or cassava litters.

**Placement.** Keep either box indoors, away from direct sun, and out of a traffic choke point. For Luma, PETLIBRO also warns against curtains and moving objects that can confuse sensors.

## Smart Features and Subscription Caveats

Both brands sell "smart" boxes. Read what is free.

**Litter-Robot 4:** The Whisker app monitors waste and litter levels, tracks weight after visits, and shows a 7-day history on the free plan. Whisker+ is optional for longer history and trend insights. There is no built-in camera on the Litter-Robot 4 (camera monitoring is a Litter-Robot 5 Pro feature in Whisker's lineup).

**Luma:** PETLIBRO's FAQ says you do **not** need Video Cloud AI to use the box. Without it you still get visit logs, times, durations, and important alerts such as no activity for 24 hours. Livestreaming is available without a subscription. AI waste analysis (pee vs poop, solid vs loose), event recording, and historical playback require Video Cloud AI. SD cards are not supported, so cloud storage is the recording path.

If you want camera-based waste analysis without paying monthly, neither free tier fully covers that. If you mainly want "stop scooping and tell me when the drawer is full," Litter-Robot 4's free app is enough for many homes.

## Running Costs to Budget For

Whichever you buy, plan beyond the sticker price:

- **Waste liners or bags** each time you empty the drawer
- **Carbon filters / deodorizers** (Whisker filters or OdorTrap; Luma carbon deodorizer—PETLIBRO suggests replacing about every 3 months in single-cat homes, or as needed)
- **Litter top-ups** as clumps are removed
- **Optional subscriptions** (Whisker+ or PETLIBRO Video Cloud AI)
- **Return windows:** Amazon's 2026 holiday return window for many items purchased Nov 1–Dec 31 extends into late January 2027—confirm on the product page before you buy

## How to Introduce Either Box

1. Place the new box next to the old one with the same litter your cat already uses (within the maker's litter rules).
2. Leave automatic cleaning off for the first few days so the cat can explore without surprise motion.
3. Reward calm visits; do not force the cat inside.
4. Only remove the old box after several days of reliable use.
5. For kittens under the maker's minimum weight, keep auto-clean off and use manual clean (Whisker automatic mode needs ≥3 lb; PETLIBRO asks you to disable auto-clean under 2.2 lb or for nursing/pregnant cats).
