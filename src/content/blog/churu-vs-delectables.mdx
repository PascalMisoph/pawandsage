**Quick answer:** Buy **[INABA Churu](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07W5R3CR6?tag=pawandsage21-20)** if you want the 6 kcal tube with INABA's listed taurine and vitamin E, and a wide flavor list. Buy **[Delectables Squeeze Up](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08XNWX31S?tag=pawandsage21-20)** if you want Hartz's 7.4 kcal tube and a pack that is easy to find in grocery and pet aisles. Both are lickable treats, not meals. We have not taste-tested them.

Official figures below were checked on November 18, 2026. Recipes vary by flavor, so read the pack you actually buy. Check the current price, and note that opened or even unopened treat packs are often non-returnable.

**Key takeaways**

- **Churu (INABA):** 6.0 kcal per tube and up to 91% moisture on the official tuna variety pack; ingredients on that pack include tuna, tapioca starch, guar gum, vitamin E, taurine and green tea extract. INABA says the treats are made without grains, preservatives, artificial colors or carrageenan.
- **Delectables Squeeze Up (Hartz):** 7.4 kcal per 0.5 oz tube, up to 90% moisture, up to three tubes a day for an average adult cat, and treats kept under 10% of daily calories, per Hartz. The official chicken recipe names cage-free chicken and wild-caught tuna and says no artificial colors, flavors or added preservatives.
- **Where Churu wins:** fewer calories per tube on the official numbers we compared, plus taurine called out on the INABA label.
- **Where Delectables wins:** slightly higher calories if you want more energy per tube, and wide retail availability that Hartz lists at Amazon, Chewy, Walmart, Target and grocery stores.
- **Both are treats.** Hand-feed, use as a topper, or (with your vet's ok) hide a pill. Keep a wand toy in the rotation too; see [best interactive cat toys](/blog/best-interactive-cat-toys).

## Calories, Moisture and Ingredients

This is the cleanest difference we can cite.

INABA's tuna variety pack lists **430 kcal/kg, 6.0 kcal per tube**, with a maximum of **91% moisture**, and a guaranteed analysis that includes taurine at a minimum of 0.025%. The tuna recipe ingredients are water, tuna, tapioca starch, natural flavors, guar gum, natural tuna flavor, vitamin E supplement, taurine and green tea extract. INABA also says Churu is made without grains, preservatives, artificial colors or carrageenan, and that the line has many flavors (it says nineteen on that page).

Hartz lists Delectables Squeeze Up Chicken at **7.4 kcal per tube** and **up to 90% moisture**. The official page says the chicken recipe is made with real cage-free chicken and real wild-caught tuna, with no artificial colors, no artificial flavors, no added preservatives, no added fillers and no added by-products. Suggested feeding is **up to three tubes a day** for an average adult cat, and all treats combined under 10% of daily calories.

Those calorie numbers are close, but they add up if a cat gets several tubes plus other snacks. If your cat needs a tight calorie budget, the official 6 kcal Churu tube is the lighter of the two figures we compared. If you want a little more energy in each serving, Squeeze Up is officially higher.

Recipes change by flavor. Hartz also sells broths and stews that are not the same as Squeeze Up tubes; some of those pouches list carrageenan on retailer pages. This comparison is about the **Squeeze Up tubes**, not the whole Delectables line.

## How to Serve Them, and When to Skip Them

Both brands want you to tear the tip and let the cat lick the tube, or squeeze a little over food. That makes them useful for shy cats, for a short bonding moment, and for cats that ignore dry treats. They are also a moisture source, which matters for cats that eat mostly kibble. They do not replace a [water fountain or bowl](/blog/cat-water-bowl-vs-fountain-summer).

Skip them, or ask a vet first, if your cat has a food allergy, needs a prescription diet, or is overweight and already at its treat budget. A tube is still a treat if you use it to hide a pill.

For holiday stockings, we already include Churu in [cat stocking stuffers](/blog/cat-stocking-stuffers). If you want a non-food extra, use a wand or a puzzle instead of a second box of tubes.
