**Quick answer:** The best **Litter-Robot alternative** for most budgets is the **[CATLINK Scooper Open-X](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FVF2V63V?tag=pawandsage21-20)**. It's an open-top, clumping-litter box CATLINK lists at $199. For large cats or a longer warranty, step up to the **[Neakasa M1 Plus](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CSKBWBF6?tag=pawandsage21-20)**. If you want the cheapest entry, the **[PetSafe ScoopFree Crystal Classic](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CFRY7VYN?tag=pawandsage21-20)** runs on crystal trays.

The [Litter-Robot 4](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B7835C46?tag=pawandsage21-20) is still the benchmark, but at $699 (Whisker's "starting at" price) it's a big bet on a machine your cat might refuse. This guide groups real alternatives by what you actually care about: price, open-top design and big cats. Specs come from maker pages checked on November 8, 2026.

**Key takeaways**

- **Under $200:** CATLINK Open-X (clumping litter, open-top) or PetSafe ScoopFree Crystal Classic (crystal trays, cats up to 15 lb).
- **Under $400:** Neakasa M1 Plus (Neakasa sale price $379.99 on Nov 8, 2026), PETKIT PuraMax 2 or PetSafe ScoopFree SmartSpin ($399.99 list).
- **Open-top:** CATLINK Open-X, Neakasa M1 Plus, PetSafe OpenSky and PETLIBRO Luma. Cornell notes most cats prefer uncovered boxes.
- **Large cats:** Neakasa M1 Plus and PetSafe OpenSky are rated to about 33 lb, more than the Litter-Robot 4's 25 lb.
- **Hidden cost:** crystal-tray boxes need proprietary trays every month. Clumping boxes just need litter and bags.

## Best Litter-Robot Alternatives Under $200

### [CATLINK Scooper Open-X](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FVF2V63V?tag=pawandsage21-20): Best Overall Budget Alternative

CATLINK lists the Open-X at **$199** on its US site. It's an open-top rotating box with a 12-liter waste drawer that CATLINK rates for up to 15 days with one cat. It takes any clumping litter, connects to 2.4 GHz or 5 GHz Wi-Fi, and uses an "incomplete gear" so the entrance never fully closes. Weight, infrared and anti-pinch sensors pause cleaning when a cat steps in.

Two catches. The standard version tracks one cat; CATLINK charges a **one-time $30** to unlock multi-cat recognition in the app. It also needs a firm, level floor (no carpet) and about 6 inches of clearance. Wirecutter picked it as its budget pick but noted a barebones app and occasional cleaning hiccups.

## Best Litter-Robot Alternatives Under $400

### [Neakasa M1 Plus](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CSKBWBF6?tag=pawandsage21-20): Best Mid-Price and Large-Cat Pick

Neakasa showed the M1 Plus at a **$379.99 sale price** (regular $549.99) on November 8, 2026. It's open-top with a 13.86-inch entry height. The 11.23-liter waste bin is rated for up to 14 days with one cat, and the weight range runs from 2.2 lb to around 33 lb. It has 6 sets of infrared sensors plus 4 weight sensors, a 2-year limited warranty and a 30-day return policy. Neakasa recommends fast-clumping litter and warns against pellets, newspaper and non-clumping types.

**Safety note:** Wirecutter dropped the original M1 as a pick after reports of cats being injured when the drum over-rotated. Neakasa updated the firmware and removed teeth from the gear in its newer model. Whatever you buy, install firmware updates.

- **[CATLINK Scooper Open-X](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FVF2V63V?tag=pawandsage21-20):** the budget open-top choice.
- **[Neakasa M1 Plus](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CSKBWBF6?tag=pawandsage21-20):** mid-price, widest weight range.
