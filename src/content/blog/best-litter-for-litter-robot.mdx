## What Litter-Robot Actually Needs

A Litter-Robot does not scoop the way you do with a hand scoop. The globe turns, a screen sifts clumps out of the clean litter, and the clumps fall into a sealed waste drawer. That only works if the urine and stool form a tight clump that is large enough to be caught and small enough to pass through the system without smearing the liner.

Whisker therefore starts from one rule: the system requires clumping litter. In the same official guide, it adds three practical filters:

1. **High-quality clay-clumping litter** makes tighter clumps, which means less leftover mess in the globe. Whisker also notes that Litter-Robot 4, 5 and EVO let you set the cycle delay to 3, 7, 15, 25 or 30 minutes, and a fast-clumping litter is what makes the short 3-minute setting realistic if you have a dog that raids the box.
2. **Low dust and a reasonable particle size** keep the LowDust screen from filling the room with a cloud each cycle.
3. **Unscented is usually better.** Whisker says scented litter masks odor instead of trapping it, and that most cats prefer unscented litter. A cat that stops using the box because of perfume is a worse problem than a faint smell.

The official "best" products — Lit Premium and Tidy Cats Designed for Litter-Robot — are both described as clay clumping litters with a uniform granule size, low dust and tight clumps. Lit is a single-ingredient sodium bentonite clay, sun-dried, with no additives, according to Whisker. Tidy Cats Designed for Litter-Robot is formulated for the machine and for Whisker's "Perfect Cycle" setting; Whisker says its fragrance is motion-activated when the unit cycles, not a constant perfume in the room.

## What to Avoid, Including the Tofu Question

The "what not to use" list matters as much as the yes list, because the wrong texture is how drawers fill with clean litter or sensors start lying.

**Pellets and wood or paper litters** — Feline Pine, Okocat, Catit pea husk, Tidy Cats Breeze, Catalyst, Yesterday's News — do not sift the way a Litter-Robot needs, according to Whisker. Catalyst gets an extra warning: Whisker says it can be small enough to go through the screen but too fine, so clean litter ends up in the drawer.

**World's Best** is a popular corn litter, and it is still on Whisker's not-recommended list. The official guide cites stronger odor, damage to the drawer-full and pinch sensors, and a higher chance of mold or pests. If you came to this page hoping to keep World's Best in a new Litter-Robot, Whisker is asking you to switch.

**Tofu litter** is not a single answer. Whisker says some tofu brands work if they clump well and the particles are the right size, and it specifically says Tuft & Paw Really Great Cat Litter will not sift properly. If tofu is non-negotiable for you, a Litter-Robot may be the wrong machine. Boxes such as the [PETKIT PuraMax 2](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DWMX6GNT?tag=pawandsage21-20) officially accept tofu and mixed litter; see our [Litter-Robot 4 vs PETKIT PuraMax 2](/blog/petkit-pura-max-2-vs-litter-robot-4) comparison before you force a litter the globe cannot handle.

**Crystal and PrettyLitter:** small silica beads can pass the screen, but translucent or white crystal litter can confuse litter-level and drawer-full readings on Litter-Robot 4, 5/Pro and EVO. PrettyLitter is listed as workable only in Litter-Robot 3.

## How to Fill the Globe, and When to Add More

Whisker says the optimal litter bed is at or just below the fill line inside the globe. The Litter-Robot 4 manual describes that as roughly 3.6 to 4.5 kg, or about 4 liters, once the litter is spread flat. Too much litter gets expelled into the waste drawer during the first cycles. Too little leaves a thin bed and weaker clumps.

Check the level each time you empty the drawer. Whisker suggests about once a week for one cat, and more often in a multi-cat home. For a household with several cats, also see our [best automatic litter boxes for multiple cats](/blog/best-automatic-litter-box-multiple-cats) guide; the litter rule stays the same, but the drawer fills faster.

LitterHopper, Whisker's add-on for Litter-Robot 4 and 5/Pro, holds 13 to 17 cups and can keep the bed at the line for more than two weeks in a one-cat home, according to the official litter guide. Whisker says it works with clay and silica but still recommends clay-clumping litter so level and waste sensors stay accurate. We do not have a verified Amazon ASIN for LitterHopper, so we are not linking one here.

## Switching Litter Without Losing the Box

If you are introducing a Litter-Robot, keep the litter your cat already uses whenever it is a compatible clumping clay. Cornell's house-soiling guidance is the reminder: cats can stop using a box when something about it changes, and a new globe plus a new smell is two changes at once.

If you must switch — for example, you are leaving World's Best or a pellet litter — change the litter in the old box first, confirm the cat still uses it, then move the new litter into the Litter-Robot. Leave automatic cycles off for the first few days so the machine is just a box. Then turn cycling on and watch the first week of drawers: you want tight clumps in the bag, not a layer of clean granules.
