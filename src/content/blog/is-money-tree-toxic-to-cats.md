**Quick answer:** No. The ASPCA lists the money tree (*Pachira aquatica*) as **non-toxic to cats, dogs and horses**. Its entry still lists **nausea, vomiting and loose stool** as possible signs, so a cat that chews a lot of it may have a mild stomach upset.

## What the ASPCA plant database says

- **Scientific name:** *Pachira aquatica*
- **Family:** Malvaceae
- **Other common names:** money plant
- **ASPCA verdict:** Non-toxic to cats
- **Clinical signs listed:** nausea, vomiting, loose stool

The verdict above comes straight from the [ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center's entry](https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/aspca-poison-control/toxic-and-non-toxic-plants/money-tree). ASPCA Poison Control is the reference most US veterinarians use for plant questions.

## Which "money plant" do you have?

This is where people get caught out. "Money tree" and "money plant" are nicknames for several unrelated plants:

- **Pachira aquatica**, the braided-trunk "money tree": non-toxic per the ASPCA.
- **Jade** (*Crassula*), sometimes called a money plant: listed as toxic ([is jade plant toxic to cats?](/blog/is-jade-plant-toxic-to-cats)).
- **Pothos** (*Epipremnum aureum*), sold as "money plant" in some regions: listed as toxic ([is pothos toxic to cats?](/blog/is-pothos-toxic-to-cats)).

Check the scientific name on the label or ask the shop before it comes home.

## What "non-toxic" means here

The ASPCA lists the toxic principles for Pachira as "None," but still records mild digestive signs. That matches the general rule: non-toxic plants are not food. If your cat eats a lot of any plant and vomits more than once, seems lethargic or stops eating, call your vet.

## When to call the vet anyway

Non-toxic means the plant is not expected to poison your cat, not that it is food. Call your veterinarian if your cat vomits repeatedly, seems lethargic, stops eating, or if you are not sure the plant really was money tree (look-alikes and nicknames cause a lot of confusion). If you think your cat ate something toxic, call the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center at [(888) 426-4435](tel:+18884264435) (a consultation fee may apply).
