**Quick answer:** Choose a hard-clumping **[clay litter such as Dr. Elsey's Ultra Unscented](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0009X0QSY?tag=pawandsage21-20)** if you want the type most cats already know, the type most stores stock, and the type most automatic boxes are built for. Choose a **[tofu litter such as pidan Natural Tofu](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B9MVXJNJ?tag=pawandsage21-20)** if you want a lighter, plant-based clumping pellet and you will follow the brand's flushing rules — or skip flushing and bag it. If the box is a Litter-Robot, read [best litter for Litter-Robot](/blog/best-litter-for-litter-robot) first; that decision is about sifting, not about this general type comparison.

We have not tested these litters in our home. Facts below come from official brand pages checked on November 18, 2026, plus Whisker's published compatibility guidance summarized in our Litter-Robot litter article. Check the current price on any listing. We do not invent cost-per-month figures.

**Key takeaways**

- **Clay still wins** on availability, a familiar texture for many cats, and compatibility with most automatic boxes, including Litter-Robot's official clay recommendation.
- **Tofu often wins** on bag weight and on brand claims about lower dust, lower tracking and (sometimes) flushing. Those are maker claims, not our lab results.
- **Flushing is conditional.** Tuft & Paw's own rules: no clumps larger than a billiard ball; no flushing if the cat is outdoor or raw-fed; check local laws. Never flush clay.
- **Switch slowly.** A new texture is a common reason cats avoid the box.
- **A third path:** crystal litters such as [PrettyLitter](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C9SKNV7S?tag=pawandsage21-20) are a different job (non-clumping silica, often sold for health monitoring). See [PrettyLitter vs Tidy Cats](/blog/pretty-litter-vs-tidy-cats).

## Weight, Dust and Tracking

Tuft & Paw says a 9.25 lb bag of [Really Great Cat Litter](https://www.awin1.com/cread.php?awinmid=59149&awinaffid=3141803&ued=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.tuftandpaw.com%2Fproducts%2Freally-great-cat-litter) is **58% lighter than clay** and lasts about a month for one cat, and it markets the litter as **99% dust-free** and ultra low-tracking. pidan markets its Natural Tofu Cat Litter as anti-dust, ultra low-tracking and fast-clumping, in 5.29 lb bags, and says one cat typically uses about two bags a month (its own estimate; habits vary).

Clay bags are heavier. That matters if you carry litter up stairs. Dust and tracking vary by brand: unscented clumping clays such as Dr. Elsey's Ultra and Fresh Step Advanced Simply Unscented are the formulas we already link as everyday clay options. We are not assigning a dust score we did not measure. If someone in the house is dust-sensitive, compare the maker's dust language and be ready to switch if the cat or a person reacts.

## Clumping, Odor and Daily Scooping

Both types can be sold as clumping litters. Clay clumps are the texture most automatic rakes and globes expect. Tofu clumps are pellets that stick together; they can feel different under a paw that grew up on clay.

Odor control is mostly about scooping, box count, and whether the formula is unscented. Many cats prefer unscented litter. If you use a scented clay or a lavender tofu, watch for avoidance. Tuft & Paw sells both an unscented charcoal formula and a lavender version; start unscented if your cat is already picky.

pidan's official ingredients list is not "only soy." The product page names soy and pea pulp fiber, a cassava-wheat-corn starch and flour blend, and a silica and calcium carbonate blend with trace components. Read the bag, especially if you chose tofu to avoid certain minerals.

## Flushing, Trash and Automatic Boxes

This is where the two types diverge most.

**Do not flush clay.** It does not belong in household plumbing.

**Tofu is not automatically toilet-safe.** Tuft & Paw says Really Great Cat Litter disintegrates in water, but it also says: do not flush clumps larger than a billiard ball; do not flush if the cat spends time outdoors or eats a raw diet (toxoplasmosis risk); and check local rules, including California and some other municipalities. pidan markets its tofu litter as flushable; follow that bag too. If you are unsure, bag solids and put them in the trash.

**Automatic boxes:** Whisker recommends a standard-weight clumping clay for Litter-Robot and lists some tofu products as only sometimes compatible. It specifically says Tuft & Paw Really Great Cat Litter will not sift properly. Other machines, such as the [PETKIT PuraMax 2](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DWMX6GNT?tag=pawandsage21-20), officially accept tofu or mixed litter. Match the litter to the machine; do not force a pellet through a globe that rejects it. Details are in [best litter for Litter-Robot](/blog/best-litter-for-litter-robot) and [Litter-Robot 4 vs PETKIT PuraMax 2](/blog/petkit-pura-max-2-vs-litter-robot-4).
