**Quick answer:** Unplug the [Litter-Robot 4](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B7835C46?tag=pawandsage21-20), press **Empty** to dump the litter into the drawer, lift off the bonnet and globe, remove the carbon filter, and wash the **globe** with water and mild soap (no bleach). Let it dry completely. **Wipe the base** with a cloth dampened with disinfectant, never spray it. Then clean the three **curtain sensors** in the bezel with a dry cloth, a vacuum brush and a cotton swab, reassemble, and press **Reset** then **Cycle**. A solid blue light means you are done. Whisker recommends a deep clean at least every 3 months.

**Key takeaways**

- **Schedule:** basic globe clean every 1 to 3 months, deep clean at least every 3 months (Whisker).
- **Globe = washable.** No electronics inside; soap and water or Whisker's cleaner, then fully dry.
- **Base and control panel = never wet.** Disinfectant on a cloth only.
- **No bleach** inside the globe.
- **Sensors cause most "drawer full" and false cat alerts.** Dust and fur on the curtain sensors are the usual suspects.

## What You Need

- Mild dish soap or Litter-Robot cleaner, and a soft sponge or cloth
- A disinfectant you can put on a cloth (not sprayed onto the base)
- Dry microfiber cloth, cotton swabs, and a vacuum with a brush attachment (compressed air optional)
- Fresh [Litter-Robot waste drawer liners](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F3QTK9ZT?tag=pawandsage21-20) and fresh clumping clay, such as [Tidy Cats Designed for Litter-Robot](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DVZD2LJR?tag=pawandsage21-20)
- A replacement carbon filter if yours is due

## Step 1: Empty the Globe

1. Press **Empty**. The globe turns so the litter falls through the waste port into the drawer.
2. Sweep any leftover litter toward the waste port.
3. Press **Cycle** or **Reset** to return the globe to Home.

Whisker's warning is worth repeating: get all the litter out before any water touches the globe, because wet litter turns into messy clay. If the unit is stuck mid-cycle, a short press of Reset finishes the cycle; a 3-second long press zeroes the weight scale if it is in an error state.

## Step 2: Unplug and Take It Apart

1. Turn the Litter-Robot off and **unplug it**.
2. Remove accessories such as the fence, shield or LitterHopper.
3. **Open the bonnet**: press and hold the buttons on both sides, then lift and rotate it open.
4. **Lift out the globe** by its handle.
5. **Remove the carbon filter** from under the globe by pushing the top edge down to free it from the hooks.
6. Pull out the waste drawer and empty it.

## Step 3: Wash the Globe

Wash the globe with Litter-Robot cleaner spray or wipes, or water and a mild soap. Whisker says there are no electronic components in the globe, so soap and water cannot damage it, and a very dirty globe can be soaked or cleaned with a hose. **Do not use bleach** or bleach-containing cleaners inside the globe; Whisker says they can cause irritating fumes.

Check the **seal strip** at the lower front of the globe while it is out. Whisker notes fur caught there can interfere with the curtain sensors when the globe rotates. Replace it if it is peeling.

Let the globe **dry completely** before you put it back.

## Step 4: Wipe the Base (Carefully)

The base holds the sensors and electronics. Whisker's rules:

- **Never submerge, spray or soak** the base or control panel.
- Wipe surfaces with a cloth **dampened** with disinfectant. If you use a spray, spray onto the cloth, away from the base.

## Step 5: Clean the Curtain Sensors

The Litter-Robot 4 has three laser "curtain" sensors in the top of the bezel, plus a weight scale in the base. They detect your cat and measure litter and waste-drawer levels. Dust or fur on them can cause false cat detections and wrong drawer readings.

1. Wipe the bezel area around the sensors with a **dry, clean cloth**.
2. **Vacuum** the inside perimeter of the bezel with a brush attachment, passing over the sensor holes.
3. For a deep clean, with globe, bonnet and drawer removed, lean the unit back so you can see into each hole and gently wipe each sensor with a **dry cotton swab**. Gentle **compressed air** is also fine. Whisker notes the dust is hard to see, so clean even if they look fine.

## Step 6: Reassemble and Test

1. Put the dry globe back on the base and reinstall the carbon filter.
2. Insert a fresh liner in the waste drawer and slide it in.
3. Close the bonnet and reinstall any fence or accessories.
4. Plug in and power on.
5. Add fresh clumping litter up to the fill line.
6. Short-press **Reset**, then **Cycle**. After the cycle, a **solid blue light** means it is ready. A flashing light means something still needs attention; see Whisker's troubleshooting pages.

## Make Cleaning Easier Next Time

- **Use the right litter.** A hard-clumping clay keeps the globe and liner cleaner. Whisker lists Tidy Cats Designed for Litter-Robot, Dr. Elsey's and Fresh Step as compatible, and corn or tofu litters as not recommended. Full list: [best litter for Litter-Robot and automatic boxes](/blog/best-litter-for-litter-robot).
- **Use Whisker's own liners.** The company says dark bags can make LR4 drawer readings inaccurate.
- **Keep the floor level and firm.** Whisker says accurate cat and weight detection needs a firm, level surface.
