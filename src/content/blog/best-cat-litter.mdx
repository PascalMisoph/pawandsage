**Key takeaways**

- **Clumping + unscented** is what most cats prefer, according to the ASPCA. That is also what controls odor best, because you remove the urine, not cover it.
- **Multiple cats?** Use a fast, hard-clumping formula and add boxes: the ASPCA's rule is one box per cat plus one.
- **Automatic box?** Stick to clumping clay. Whisker lists corn (World's Best) and tofu (Really Great Cat Litter) as not recommended for Litter-Robot.
- **Low dust and tracking** is where tofu and corn litters beat most clay.
- **No litter fixes a dirty box.** Scoop at least daily and wash the box regularly.

## How to Choose the Right Litter

1. **Start from your cat, not the marketing.** The ASPCA notes your cat may prefer the litter type it used as a kitten. If the box is working, change slowly or not at all.
2. **Clumping beats non-clumping for odor.** You remove the whole urine clump instead of letting it soak into the base layer.
3. **Unscented first.** Cornell lists litter a cat dislikes among the common causes of house soiling, and strong scent is an easy thing to rule out.
4. **Match the box.** Automatic box = clumping clay from the maker's list. High-sided or open-top box = almost anything. If your cat pees over the edge, the box matters more than the litter: see [best litter box for cats that pee high](/blog/best-litter-box-for-cats-that-pee-high).
5. **Depth:** most cats prefer one to two inches, per the ASPCA.

## Best Cat Litter for Multiple Cats: The Setup That Matters

Litter alone will not fix a multi-cat odor problem. What works:

- **Enough boxes:** one per cat plus one, in more than one room.
- **Daily scooping**, and a full change plus wash on a routine. The ASPCA suggests rinsing the box with baking soda or unscented soap once a week.
- **A fast-clumping formula** (Fresh Step Multi Cat, World's Best Multiple Cat, OdorStop) so clumps hold together when the next cat digs.
- **Consider an automatic box** if you cannot scoop several times a day: [best automatic litter box for multiple cats](/blog/best-automatic-litter-box-multiple-cats).
- **Hide boxes in style** instead of in a closet your cats avoid: [best litter box furniture](/blog/best-litter-box-furniture).

## How to Switch Litter Without a Strike

Mix the new litter into the old over one to two weeks, adding a little more each time you top up. Tuft & Paw suggests a slower four-week mix for its tofu pellets. If your cat starts going elsewhere, go back a step. Sudden refusals can also be medical, so call your vet if a cat that always used the box stops.
