- **Catit Flower Fountain:** 3 L (100 fl oz), three flow settings, Triple Action filter, water-level window, BPA-free plastic, 8.3 × 8.3 × 7.3 in, per Catit.

## Capacity and Refills

Catit lists the Flower Fountain at **3 liters (100 fl oz)** in a compact 8.3 × 8.3 × 7.3 in body that weighs 1.32 lb. PetSafe lists the Drinkwell Original at **50 oz**, aimed at cats and small dogs. If you have two cats, or you forget mid-week refills, the Catit reservoir is the more practical of the two classics.

The Stainless 360 jumps to **128 oz**, with 14.3 × 14.3 × 9.3 in dimensions on PetSafe's page. That is the better match when several pets share one fountain. It is also larger and will not disappear into a tight corner the way the Flower Fountain can.

## Flow, Noise and Picky Drinkers

Catit says the Flower Fountain can be set three ways: a gentle flow, a bubbling top, or calm streams, so you can match a fussy drinker. PetSafe's Original uses a **free-falling stream** (its support page describes a 5-inch fall) and a receiving ramp meant to cut splash. The Stainless 360 goes further: five spout attachments and a twistable top to change stream strength.

We cannot honestly rate noise without a test. What you can do is set the flow low at first, place the fountain where you will hear a dry pump, and keep a bowl down for the first week. Our [cat water bowl vs fountain](/blog/cat-water-bowl-vs-fountain-summer) guide covers when a bowl is still enough.

## Filters, Materials and Cleaning

Catit says only filtered water is pushed to the surface, through a **Triple Action Filter** that it describes as softening water and reducing odors, impurities, hair and debris. PetSafe's Original uses a replaceable **activated carbon filter** and a pre-filter; the Stainless 360 adds a foam filter for hair and debris.

On materials, the two starter fountains are BPA-free plastic. Some cats do better with stainless or ceramic at the drinking surface; PetSafe even notes in the Original manual that some animals are sensitive to plastic bowls. If that is your issue, skip both plastic models and look at the [Stainless 360](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B002SB91QS?tag=pawandsage21-20) or the stainless cordless picks in [best cat water fountains](/blog/best-cat-water-fountains-cats-not-drinking-enough).

PetSafe states the Original is top-shelf dishwasher safe and the Stainless 360 is top-rack dishwasher safe, with the pump washed by hand. Take the maker's pump instructions seriously: a pump that runs dry will fail.

## Where Each One Wins

**Catit Flower Fountain wins** on capacity, flow variety, and the water-level window. It is the better default for most one- or two-cat homes that want a compact plastic fountain.

**PetSafe Drinkwell Original wins** if your cat is clearly drawn to a falling stream, you want a smaller volume, and dishwasher-safe parts matter to you.

**The Stainless 360 wins** when you have several pets, you want stainless steel, or you want to drink from any side of an open bowl. It is the "neither of the two classics" option, not a like-for-like swap for the 50 oz Original.
