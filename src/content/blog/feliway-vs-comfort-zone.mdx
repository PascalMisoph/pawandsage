**Quick answer:** Buy **[Feliway Classic](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08962PMQS?tag=pawandsage21-20)** if the main signs are spraying, scratching or hiding, or you also want the spray for a carrier. Buy **[Feliway Optimum](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B097W651NJ?tag=pawandsage21-20)** if stress signs are broader. Buy **[Feliway MultiCat](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GHCXCGTF?tag=pawandsage21-20)** or **[Comfort Zone Multicat](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B014HCXECW?tag=pawandsage21-20)** if the problem is tension between cats. A diffuser is a support, not a diagnosis.

Holiday guests, a new tree and a changed routine are exactly the situations Feliway lists for Classic or Optimum. If your cats already stare and block each other, MultiCat is the product Feliway points to. We have not tested these plugins in our home. What follows is Feliway's own product map, Comfort Zone's Multicat listing, and one peer-reviewed pilot study.

## How the Feliway Line Splits, in Feliway's Own Guide

Feliway's U.S. "which should I use" page is the cleanest official map.

**Classic** is the original diffuser and spray. Feliway says it supports urine spraying, scratching and hiding, and that the spray is useful in a carrier, in the car and at the vet. For house soiling, Feliway also says you still have to find the reason — a moved box or a new litter, for example — clean the area, and then use the diffuser (and optionally the spray) as support.

**Optimum** is positioned as the newer, broader pheromone complex. Feliway lists urine spraying, scratching, hiding, fighting, and stress-related reduced appetite or overgrooming. It recommends Optimum (or Classic) when you introduce a new cat, move house, prepare for a baby, or change the routine or the furniture. Each Optimum refill is described as 30 days.

**MultiCat** is the interpersonal product. Feliway says that if you have two or more cats and they are not getting along, MultiCat can help them be more tolerant, and that it supports general aggression including fighting, chasing, blocking and staring. This is also the formula historically sold as Feliway Friends, which matters for the study below.

Comfort Zone does not publish an equally detailed public "which formula when" page that we can cite here. The **[Comfort Zone Multicat Diffuser Kit](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B014HCXECW?tag=pawandsage21-20)** is the verified Amazon kit sold for cat calming in multi-cat homes. Treat it as an alternative in that slot, not as a proven clone of MultiCat.

## What the 2018 Pilot Study Actually Showed

A 2018 paper in the *Journal of Feline Medicine and Surgery* (PubMed 29757071) was a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled **pilot** of an appeasing-pheromone diffuser versus placebo for feline aggression in multi-cat households. The product under study was Feliway Friends. The trial involved 45 households.

That is real evidence, and it is also limited: it is a pilot, it tested one Feliway formula, and it did not compare Feliway with Comfort Zone. It does not mean every cat will stop fighting in a week, and it does not mean Classic or Optimum were in the protocol. When a brand says a multi-cat product was "scientifically proven," this study is the kind of source you should be able to open and read.

Use it as a reason to try MultiCat *after* a vet check and an environment fix, not as a promise.

## Holiday Guests, Fireworks and a Changed House

The ASPCA's holiday-safety page reminds owners that loud music, fireworks and noisemakers can scare pets, and that cats need a quiet interior room they can retreat to. A diffuser can be part of that room. It will not make a hallway full of strangers feel like a den.

If the issue is the *household's* routine changing — visitors, a tree, furniture shifted — Feliway points to Classic or Optimum. If the issue is cats taking it out on each other, MultiCat (or Comfort Zone Multicat) is the better match. You can also pair a calm room with the setup in [can you leave a cat alone for a weekend](/blog/can-i-leave-my-cat-alone-for-a-weekend) if you will be in and out of the house over Thanksgiving.

Do not plug a diffuser under a shelf, behind a curtain or next to a fan or an open window if the maker says that wastes the vapor. Follow the insert: usually a socket in the room where the cat spends the most time, left on continuously for the refill period.

## Neither? Fix the House First

A diffuser will not make up for one litter box, one food bowl and no high perch in a three-cat home. Give each cat a way to eat, toilet and hide without passing a rival. Our [indoor cat enrichment ideas](/blog/indoor-cat-enrichment-ideas) and [do single cats get lonely](/blog/do-single-cats-get-lonely) guides cover the environmental side. If spraying started after a litter change, see [best litter for Litter-Robot](/blog/best-litter-for-litter-robot) only if you actually have a Whisker box; otherwise go back to a litter the cat already accepted.

If the behavior is new, skip the diffuser aisle and call the vet. Pain, a urinary problem or a gastrointestinal issue can look like "holiday stress." A pheromone product is an adjunct after that check, not a first-line treatment.
