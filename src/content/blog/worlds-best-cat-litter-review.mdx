**Quick verdict:** [World's Best Multiple Cat Unscented](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07DGYXWX1?tag=pawandsage21-20) is the formula to buy for most homes: corn-based, clumping, unscented and marketed as 99% dust-free. Pick **[Low Tracking & Dust Control](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09KX9G8K3?tag=pawandsage21-20)** if tracking is the problem, or **[Multiple Cat Lavender](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0747PQL2L?tag=pawandsage21-20)** if your cat is fine with a light scent. **Skip it** if you use a Litter-Robot or a similar globe box: Whisker lists World's Best as not recommended. In that case, use a clay from our [best cat litter](/blog/best-cat-litter) list.

**Key takeaways**

- **Made from whole-kernel corn** in the USA, per the maker, with no clay dust and no artificial perfumes.
- **Maker claims:** quick-clumping, 99% dust-free, lightweight, and lasting longer than the leading U.S. brand.
- **Flushable with limits:** one to two clumps at a time in well-maintained systems; California discourages flushing feces.
- **Not for Litter-Robot,** according to Whisker.
- **Unscented first.** The ASPCA says most cats prefer clumping, unscented litter.

## Odor and Clumping

World's Best says corn traps smells deep inside the litter and absorbs waste before it sticks to the box, so you do not have to chisel. The brand also says one bag lasts longer than the leading U.S. brand and, on its homepage, that the litter is up to 60% more absorbent than traditional clay. Those are the maker's comparisons, not our measurements. Whatever you buy, the biggest odor lever is daily scooping and enough boxes (one per cat plus one, per the ASPCA).

## Dust and Tracking

Corn is naturally free of clay dust, which is the main reason people switch from cheap clay. The maker advises not to mix it with non-clumping litter. If litter on the floor is your main complaint, the **Low Tracking & Dust Control** formula uses larger granules, which the listing says keeps litter in the box. Larger tofu pellets are the other low-tracking route; see [Really Great Cat Litter review](/blog/really-great-cat-litter-review).

## Flushing: The Fine Print

World's Best says its litter is designed to flush in well-maintained systems, one to two clumps at a time, and adds that the state of California encourages trash disposal of cat feces and discourages flushing. Practical rules: flush small amounts only, never flush if your cat goes outdoors, and bag it if your plumbing or septic system is old or you are not sure. For a wider look at flushing and disposal, read [tofu vs clay cat litter](/blog/tofu-vs-clay-cat-litter).

## World's Best and Automatic Litter Boxes

This is the deal-breaker for many buyers. Whisker's official Litter-Robot compatibility guide lists World's Best as **not recommended**, citing stronger odor, damage to the drawer-full and pinch sensors, and a higher chance of mold or pests. If you are buying a Litter-Robot or already own one, switch to a clumping clay first. Our [best litter for Litter-Robot and automatic boxes](/blog/best-litter-for-litter-robot) guide has the full list, and [how to clean a Litter-Robot 4](/blog/how-to-clean-litter-robot-4) covers what to do if corn litter has already gummed up the globe.

## Who Should Buy It, and Who Should Not

**Buy it if** you scoop by hand, want a lighter bag than clay, dislike clay dust, and your cat accepts a coarser natural texture.

**Skip it if** you use a self-cleaning globe box, want the cheapest litter per month, or your cat refuses anything but fine clay.
