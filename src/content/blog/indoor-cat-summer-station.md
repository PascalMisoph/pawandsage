A summer station is the opposite of buying random cat gear.

It is one calm, practical setup that makes hot-day care easier: water, cleanup, grooming, and low-energy enrichment in places you will actually use them.

## 1. Start With Water

The water station is the core. Use one main source and at least one backup bowl. Keep water away from food and litter when possible, and place it somewhere your cat naturally passes.

For multi-pet homes or heavy-use stations, the [Pokpet 10L Stainless Cordless Fountain](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GZK62Q31?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.28ARQ0VAF4FYU&linkCode=tr1&tag=pawandsage21-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.28ARQ0VAF4FYU_1782828401574) is the higher-capacity option with a stainless basin, motion sensing, cordless placement, and multiple water modes.

## 2. Add a Grooming Corner

Keep a brush, towel, and treats together. Summer shedding becomes easier when brushing is short and predictable.

**What helps:** The [ZeaCotio Pet Glove Wipes](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GTV5M7KV?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.10C0IU46OUTC6&linkCode=tr1&tag=pawandsage21-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.10C0IU46OUTC6_1782828621824) are useful for small paw, coat, face, or rear-area cleanup after brushing, travel, or litter tracking. Use them as convenience wipes, not medical care.

## 3. Keep One Low-Energy Game Ready

Hot afternoons are not the time for frantic chase play. Use scent, food work, or a short puzzle instead.

**What helps:** The [NHCASURUE Hanging Door-Frame Treat Dispenser](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GVSZWH2B?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.1EX7RUBYS4MHA&linkCode=tr1&tag=pawandsage21-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.1EX7RUBYS4MHA_1782828550693) fits a short supervised hunting puzzle. Check the attachment and inspect it regularly.

## 4. Add a Cool Resting Choice

The station should point your cat toward a cooler room: shaded floor, tile, cooling mat, or a perch away from direct sun. If your cat likes height, a wall route or shelf near a shaded window can help them observe without overheating.

## 5. Put a Note on the Station

Write the summer basics where a sitter or tired future you can see them:

- Refresh water.
- Check backup bowl.
- Close sunny blinds.
- Watch for panting, drooling, weakness, or sudden lethargy.
- Call a vet if heat stress signs appear.
