## How to Choose a Modern Cat Tree

1. **Measure your cat lying stretched out.** Platforms should be at least that long, or your cat will not use them.
2. **Check the base.** Wider and heavier is steadier. A tall tree on a small base wobbles.
3. **Look for a weight rating** if your cat is big. No rating usually means "average cat."
4. **Scratching surface.** Sisal or jute posts take the wear and keep claws off the wood. The ASPCA notes all cats want a sturdy surface that will not shift.
5. **A hiding place.** Cubes and condos give shy cats a retreat, which the AAFP/ISFM guidelines treat as a core need.
6. **Placement.** Next to a window turns the tree into "cat TV"; see [cat window perch ideas](/blog/cat-window-perch).
