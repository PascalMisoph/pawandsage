**Quick answer:** Buy the **[PETLIBRO Granary 5L WiFi feeder](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DRBCCK7V?tag=pawandsage21-20)** if you want app control, a low-food sensor and a 5 L hopper with up to 10 meals a day. Buy the **[PetSafe Healthy Pet Simply Feed](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00VIXRB6O?tag=pawandsage21-20)** if you want a 24-cup, battery-operated feeder you program on the device, with up to 12 meals a day and a slow-release option. Both are **dry-food** machines. Neither replaces a sitter.

Automatic feeders are at their best on a normal weekday: breakfast goes down while you are on a call, or dinner happens at the usual time when you are late. They are at their worst when someone treats them as a weekend-away plan. We have not run a weeks-long reliability test in our kitchen. The comparison below uses the verified Amazon titles for these two models, plus common-sense limits from the San Francisco SPCA and Ohio State's Indoor Pet Initiative.

## PETLIBRO Granary 5L WiFi: Who It Is For

The **[PETLIBRO Granary](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DRBCCK7V?tag=pawandsage21-20)** is the connected option in this pair. The Amazon title we verified describes a **5 L** automatic feeder with **5 GHz Wi-Fi**, **freshness preservation** language, a **low-food sensor**, and **up to 10 meals a day**.

That combination is useful if you already live in your phone: you can check that a meal was scheduled, and a low-food sensor is a better reminder than hoping you remember to look in the hopper on Friday. It is less useful if your Wi-Fi is unreliable, if you only have a band the feeder does not support, or if you do not want another app.

We are not adding battery-backup hours, portion-gram charts, or "works fully offline" claims that are not on the verified title. Read the current manual for power-failure behavior before you trust the unit for a long day at work. If the power or network going down is your main worry, the PetSafe model's battery-operated, on-device programming is the more transparent design.

PETLIBRO also makes other products, including the Luma litter box we cover in our [Litter-Robot 4 vs PETLIBRO Luma](/blog/litter-robot-4-vs-petlibro-luma) guide. This page is only about the Granary 5 L feeder. Do not mix those spec sheets.

## PetSafe Simply Feed: Who It Is For

The **[PetSafe Healthy Pet Simply Feed](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00VIXRB6O?tag=pawandsage21-20)** is the older, more button-forward design, and that is its appeal. The verified listing describes a **24-cup** feeder, **up to 12 meals a day**, portions from **1/8 cup to 4 cups**, a **slow-release** option, and **battery operation**.

Twelve meals is the higher daily count in this comparison, which matters if a veterinarian has you splitting food into many small portions. The slow-release option is the feature to look at if your cat gulps and then begs. The 24-cup hopper is the larger listed capacity of the two. And because you program it on the device and it is sold as battery operated, you are not depending on an app store login to breakfast.

The tradeoff is the missing "brain": no low-food push notification, no remote meal from a train. You look in the hopper, and you change the schedule with your hands. For a lot of households, that is a feature.

We already mention this feeder in our [weekend-alone guide](/blog/can-i-leave-my-cat-alone-for-a-weekend) for the same reason: it can keep dry-food times steady. It still does not plate wet food, and it still does not replace a person walking through the door.

## Setup Habits That Matter More Than the Brand

Place the feeder where the cat already likes to eat, on a stable surface, away from the litter box. If you also run a fountain, keep water and kibble far enough apart that kibble dust does not fall into the bowl; our [where to put a cat water fountain](/blog/where-to-put-cat-water-fountain) guide covers that layout.

Start with the cat's current meal times and current portion, not a new diet on the same day you introduce a machine. Watch the first week in person. If kibble piles up, the portion is too large or the cat is unwell — do not assume the motor "owes" a second drop.

In a multi-cat home, a single open hopper can become a resource fight. Ohio State's Indoor Pet Initiative treats food, water and litter as resources each cat should access without being blocked. You may need a closed door, a baby gate, or staggered rooms more than you need a smarter app.

A feeder also does not replace play. A timed breakfast plus a [winter enrichment session](/blog/winter-enrichment-indoor-cats) is a complete morning. A timed breakfast plus eight hours of a closed apartment is just a meal.

If you travel, pair any feeder with a person and, if you like, a [camera](/blog/furbo-vs-petcube). Thanksgiving week is a classic moment to over-trust a machine. The cat still needs fresh water, a scooped box and a human who can tell that the hopper jammed.
