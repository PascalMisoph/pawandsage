## How to Read "Supports X Cats" on a Product Page

Makers count cats in different ways.

**A hard maximum**, like Whisker's "works for up to 4 cats" on the Litter-Robot 4, is the figure to trust first. Whisker's FAQ also warns that too many cats on one machine can leave the bed dirty for the next user, because a cat who enters during the wait time resets the cycle timer (the default wait is 7 minutes).

**Weight range** is a second limit. Two 12-lb cats fit a box rated to 22 lb each; a 28-lb Maine Coon does not, even if you only have one other cat.

**Recognition count** is a third, and it is the one that is easiest to over-read. "Recognizes up to 10 cats" means the app can tell them apart, not that the waste bin was designed as a 10-cat toilet.

**Drawer time** is usually quoted for one cat. Two cats will fill it roughly twice as fast, and three cats faster still, depending on diet and habits.

Cornell's Feline Health Center still matters in a multi-cat home: cats that face a dirty, blocked or frightening box may eliminate elsewhere. An automatic box helps with the "dirty" part. It does not fix a bully at the entrance.

The **[Litter-Robot 4](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B7835C46?tag=pawandsage21-20)** is the default recommendation because Whisker publishes a clear cap (four cats), a clear weight range (3 to 25 lb), a 15.75-inch entryway, litter- and waste-level sensing, and SmartScale weight tracking in the free app (7-day history; Whisker+ is optional). ## Best Open-Top Multi-Cat Options

Some cats will not walk into a globe. Cornell notes that many cats prefer uncovered boxes. In that case, do not force the Litter-Robot 4; pick an open-top machine whose weight range fits your largest cat.

**[PETLIBRO Luma](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0G599SG3B?tag=pawandsage21-20)** is the open-top smart pick when you want camera recognition. PETLIBRO lists recognition for up to 10 cats and a range of about 2.2 to 22 lb, with a 2.9-gallon waste drawer and a hard-floor requirement. Livestream has been free; AI analysis and recording have needed an optional Video Cloud AI plan. Treat "up to 10" as an ID feature, and size the box to your real group and the drawer.

**[Neakasa M1 Plus](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CSKBWBF6?tag=pawandsage21-20)** is the large-cat open-top. Neakasa rates it for 2.2 to around 33 lb, with an 11.23-liter bin (up to 14 days for one cat) and weight-based tracking in the app. Wirecutter documented over-rotation issues on the original M1 and said Neakasa addressed them with firmware and a redesigned gear; keep any M1 on current firmware. See [Litter-Robot vs Neakasa M1](/blog/litter-robot-vs-neakasa-m1) for the full comparison.

**[CATLINK Scooper Open-X](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FVF2V63V?tag=pawandsage21-20)** is the budget open-top, listed at $199 by CATLINK earlier in November 2026, with a 12-liter drawer and a 3.3 to 22 lb range. Multi-cat recognition is a one-time $30 app unlock, and CATLINK has said the cats need at least a 0.9 lb weight gap or the app may mix them up. Hard, level floor, about 6 inches of clearance. It is a reasonable two-medium-cat box, not a four-cat Litter-Robot substitute.

**[PetSafe ScoopFree OpenSky](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DTHVL2DW?tag=pawandsage21-20)** is another large-cat open-top: up to 33 lb, 8.5-liter drawer, clumping litter, and a 1-to-60-minute cleaning delay in the app. PetSafe listed it at $549.99 earlier in November 2026.

The **[PetSafe ScoopFree SmartSpin](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D4GHS7V6?tag=pawandsage21-20)** sits in the middle: up to 25 lb, a 6-liter drawer (up to two weeks for one cat), clumping litter, and app tracking with no subscription, at a $399.99 list price when we checked petsafe.com in early November 2026. Fine for two average cats; tight for three busy ones.

## Litter, Placement and a Second Box

Use the litter the machine is designed for. Whisker boxes want clumping clay; our [best litter for Litter-Robot](/blog/best-litter-for-litter-robot) guide follows the official yes/no list. Luma, Neakasa, CATLINK, PuraMax 2 and OpenSky have their own rules, which we already cite on those comparison pages.

Place the box where every cat can reach it without walking past a bully, on the floor type the maker allows (several of the open-top and PETKIT boxes want hard floors). In a four-cat home, one perfectly rated Litter-Robot 4 plus a simple backup box in a second room is often calmer than insisting a single machine must do all the work.

Introduce the new box beside the old ones, keep familiar litter when it is compatible, and leave automatic cycles off for a few days. A multi-cat introduction is slower than a single-cat one, because any cat can decide the new object is a problem.
