## Ingredients: Closer Than the Marketing Suggests

Both litters are made from soy fiber, a by-product of soy processing, plus pea fiber and starch to bind the pellets. The meaningful difference is the odor helper: Tuft & Paw's unscented version uses activated carbon, while pidan's ingredient list names a silica and calcium carbonate blend. Neither is "only tofu." If you chose tofu to avoid a specific mineral or grain, read both labels.

## Bag Size, Weight and Monthly Use

This is where buyers often get the math wrong. A pidan bag is lighter and smaller, but pidan's own estimate is about two bags per cat per month. Tuft & Paw estimates one 9.25 lb bag per cat per month. So compare the price of a month's supply at the moment you buy, including any subscription discount, not the price per bag.

If you carry litter up stairs, both are far lighter than clay. Tuft & Paw says its bag is 58% lighter than clay.

## Dust, Tracking and Odor

Both makers claim low dust and low tracking. We have not measured either, so we will not rank them on those claims. Practical differences:

- **Odor options:** Tuft & Paw has three routes, including the [OdorStop](https://www.awin1.com/cread.php?awinmid=59149&awinaffid=3141803&ued=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.tuftandpaw.com%2Fproducts%2Freally-great-cat-litter-odorstop) blend for multi-cat homes. pidan has one core formula.
- **Scent:** if your cat is scent-sensitive, both have unscented options. Skip Lavender if your cat is picky.

## Flushing and Automatic Boxes

Both are marketed as flushable. Follow the limits on each bag, check local rules, and do not flush waste from outdoor or raw-fed cats. OdorStop contains bentonite and is not a flush product.

For automatic boxes: Whisker lists Really Great Cat Litter as not recommended for Litter-Robot because it does not sift properly, and Tuft & Paw says the same about most self-cleaning boxes. We have no official Whisker approval for pidan either. If you own a Litter-Robot, stay with clay; see [best litter for Litter-Robot](/blog/best-litter-for-litter-robot).
