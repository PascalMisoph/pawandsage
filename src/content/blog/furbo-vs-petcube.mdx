## Furbo 360° Cat Camera: What the Official Page Says

Furbo's U.S. comparison page lists a **360° Cat Camera** as designed for cats, with "colors your pet can see" and a feather wand in the box on some kits. Official hardware claims include 1080p video, a rotating 360° view with 4x digital zoom, auto tracking (it rotates and zooms to follow the pet), treat tossing with a listed capacity of 100 treats, a pet-proof design, tabletop placement, and a size of about 8.8 × 4.9 × 4.9 inches at 1.7 pounds. Furbo describes it as a fit for an open space such as a living room.

That is the camera we mean when we say "Furbo" in this guide. Furbo also sells dog models, a Mini, and wide-angle add-on cameras. Do not mix those spec sheets.

**About subscriptions:** Furbo's site describes a Furbo Nanny subscription for extra alerts, video playback and behavior reports. That is separate from the hardware. The Amazon listing we use is the **[Furbo 360° Cat Camera with core features and no ongoing cost](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C4XXBY44?tag=pawandsage21-20)**. Other Furbo listings on Amazon clearly say a subscription is required. If you only want the core live view and treat toss without a monthly plan, stay on the "no ongoing cost" title and read the current bullet list at checkout. We do not promise which alerts remain free six months from now; makers change plans.

Treat toss is fun and it is also a calorie source. Use a treat your vet already allows, in a small amount, and do not fire a stream of treats at a cat that will sit under the camera all afternoon. A camera treat is not a meal, and it is not a substitute for a [wand session](/blog/best-interactive-cat-toys) when you get home.

## Petcube Cam: What the Official Page Says

Petcube Cam is the simple, small camera. Official specs from Petcube's product page (checked November 2026):

- 1080p HD video, 8x digital zoom, 110° wide-angle lens
- Two-way full-duplex audio
- Night vision up to 30 feet
- 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only; a broadband connection with about 2 Mbps upload is recommended
- About 60.6 × 54 × 81.78 mm (2.39 × 2.13 × 3.22 in)
- In the box: camera, 2 m USB cable, power adapter, adhesive tape, quick-start guide
- 1-year limited warranty on Petcube's site

Petcube's own FAQ says Cam **does not rotate, pan or tilt**. You turn the body to aim it. That is the main hardware tradeoff versus Furbo.

**About subscriptions:** Petcube is clearer than many camera brands. The page says you can use Cam out of the box with **no required monthly fee**. Without optional **Petcube Care**, you will not get video storage or smart alerts. Care is described as adding a video timeline, smart alerts, Daily Diary-style clips and browser playback. We are not printing a plan price here because those numbers change; check Petcube's Care page when you buy.

If you only want to open an app, see the room, and talk, Petcube Cam is built for that without a plan. If you want a day's worth of clips after something happens, budget for Care or choose a camera whose current listing includes the history you need.

## Where to Put It, and What a Camera Cannot Do

Point the lens at the places your cat actually uses: the window, the tree, the hallway to the litter box — not a decorative shelf that films a blank wall. For Petcube, aim it once and use the wide angle. For Furbo, place it on a stable table in an open room, out of a leaping path so the treat hopper is not a climbing toy.

Use two-way audio sparingly. A sudden voice from a speaker can scare some cats more than it comforts them. A short check-in is enough.

A camera will not:

- Scoop the box or refill a fountain
- Notice a urinary blockage if you never open the app
- Replace the 12-hour check the San Francisco SPCA recommends
- Make a holiday house quieter; for guests and fireworks, still offer a closed quiet room, as the ASPCA suggests, and consider a [calming diffuser](/blog/feliway-vs-comfort-zone) as a support

If you also want food to go out on a timer, that is a feeder problem, not a camera problem. See [PETLIBRO vs PetSafe automatic feeders](/blog/petlibro-vs-petsafe-automatic-feeder). And if you are buying a camera as a gift, pair it with realistic expectations — it is a window into the room, not a nanny.
