**Quick answer:** Buy the **[PETKIT PuraMax 2](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DWMX6GNT?tag=pawandsage21-20)** if your cats are small to medium, the box will sit on a hard floor, and you want a lower-priced smart box that can use clay or tofu litter. Buy the **[Litter-Robot 4](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B7835C46?tag=pawandsage21-20)** if you have larger cats, up to four cats, or you want Whisker's 90-day in-home trial and the longer track record.

Both machines sift clumping litter into a sealed waste drawer so you stop scooping every day. They differ in size, litter rules, how many cats the makers officially support, and what you pay up front. Specs below come from Whisker's comparison chart and product page and from PETKIT's 2026 buying guide and PuraMax 2 user manual, checked in November 2026.

**Key takeaways**

- **Price:** Whisker lists the Litter-Robot 4 starting at $699. PETKIT calls the PuraMax 2 its most cost-effective smart box; check today's live price rather than an old screenshot.
- **Cat size:** Litter-Robot 4 is rated for 3 to 25 lb with a 15.75-inch entryway. PuraMax 2 is rated for 3.3 to 22 lb with a 7.87-inch entrance.
- **Cats supported:** Whisker rates the Litter-Robot 4 for up to four cats. PETKIT positions the PuraMax 2 for one cat or a small multi-cat home and does not publish a maximum count.
- **Waste:** Whisker says empty about every eight days for one cat. PETKIT lists a 7-liter bin rated for up to 15 days for one cat.
- **Litter:** Litter-Robot 4 wants clumping clay. PuraMax 2 accepts clumping clay, tofu or mixed litter and includes two sifters.
- **Floor:** PETKIT's manual requires a hard, flat surface. The Litter-Robot 4 can sit on carpet if you add Whisker's Carpet Tray.

## Litter-Robot 4 vs PETKIT PuraMax 2: Size and Large Cats

This is the decision that matters most if you have a big cat.

The **[Litter-Robot 4](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B7835C46?tag=pawandsage21-20)** is an enclosed globe. Whisker lists a 15.75-inch entryway, a 6.9-inch step and a 16.5-inch interior height, and rates it for cats from 3 to 25 lb. That is the same official range we used in our [Litter-Robot 4 vs 5 vs EVO](/blog/litter-robot-4-vs-5-vs-evo) and [Litter-Robot 4 vs Petlibro Luma](/blog/litter-robot-4-vs-petlibro-luma) guides. A Maine Coon or other large breed at the top of that range is a documented fit on paper; a cat over 25 lb is not.

The **[PETKIT PuraMax 2](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DWMX6GNT?tag=pawandsage21-20)** is smaller in the ways that affect a tall cat. PETKIT's 2026 buying guide lists a 7.87-inch entrance height and a 3.3 to 22 lb weight range. The official user manual adds that the box is for cats older than six months and is not recommended under 1.5 kg (3.3 lb). The low entrance can be a plus for senior or short-legged cats, which is why we flagged it that way in our [Litter-Robot alternatives](/blog/litter-robot-alternatives) guide. It is a weaker match for a 20-plus-pound cat that needs more headroom.

If your cat is over 22 lb, or simply refuses a tight cylinder, skip this head-to-head and look at an open-top box such as the [Neakasa M1 Plus](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CSKBWBF6?tag=pawandsage21-20) in our [Litter-Robot vs Neakasa M1](/blog/litter-robot-vs-neakasa-m1) comparison.

## Litter-Robot 4 vs PETKIT PuraMax 2: How Many Cats, and the Waste Drawer

Whisker is specific: the Litter-Robot 4 "works for up to 4 cats," and the waste drawer typically needs emptying about every eight days in a one-cat home. With more cats, you empty more often. Whisker also notes that if another cat enters before the cycle finishes, the timer resets, so a busy box can leave waste in the litter longer.

PETKIT is less specific on cat count. Its 2026 buying guide places the PuraMax 2 in "single or two-cat households" and "single-cat or small multi-cat households," and it does not publish a maximum number of cats the way Whisker does. What PETKIT does publish is capacity: a 7-liter waste bin rated for up to 15 days for one cat, with manual waste packing (you bag and empty the bin yourself). Those 15 days are a lab-style figure for one cat; two cats will fill it faster.

If you have three or four cats, the Litter-Robot 4 is the box with a clear official rating. For a deeper multi-cat rundown, see our guide to the [best automatic litter boxes for multiple cats](/blog/best-automatic-litter-box-multiple-cats).

## Litter-Robot 4 vs PETKIT PuraMax 2: Litter Rules

Litter is where these two boxes really diverge, and it is also a recurring cost.

**[Litter-Robot 4](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B7835C46?tag=pawandsage21-20):** Whisker recommends a high-quality, standard-weight clumping clay. It says plant-based litters typically do not filter properly, and that non-clumping, loose-clumping, newspaper and wood-pellet litters do not work. Some silica beads or crystals can pass the screen, but translucent or white crystal litter can confuse the litter-level and drawer-full sensors, so Whisker does not recommend it. If you want a litter made for the machine, [Purina Tidy Cats Designed for Litter-Robot](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DVZD2LJR?tag=pawandsage21-20) is the Amazon option we already use on the site. Our [best litter for Litter-Robot](/blog/best-litter-for-litter-robot) guide walks through Whisker's full yes/no list.

**[PETKIT PuraMax 2](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DWMX6GNT?tag=pawandsage21-20):** PETKIT says the box works with clumping clay, tofu and mixed litter. The user manual explains that a standard sifter is meant for tofu and mixed litter, and a honeycomb fine sifter is meant for mineral or clay litter. PETKIT advises against litter longer than 12 mm. That flexibility matters if you already use tofu litter or want to avoid clay dust. It does not mean every tofu pellet on the market will sift cleanly; particle size still matters.

Do not switch litter and box at the same time if you can help it. A new machine is already a change. Keep the litter your cat already accepts, as long as it fits the maker's rules.

## Litter-Robot 4 vs PETKIT PuraMax 2: Odor, App and Floor Placement

Both boxes deposit waste into a sealed bin or drawer instead of leaving clumps in an open tray.

The Litter-Robot 4 is a closed globe with a waste drawer, carbon filter and optional Whisker+ history. The free app shows seven days of usage and weight data through SmartScale. Whisker also sells liners and filters as recurring supplies.

The PuraMax 2, in PETKIT's own comparison table, has "triple odor control" and manual waste packing, but no camera, no audio and no AI multi-cat recognition — those features sit on PETKIT's more expensive Purobot models. The app can still log usage and send 24-hour inactivity notifications where supported. Wi-Fi is 2.4 GHz only, same band the Whisker app uses.

Floor placement is easy to miss in a spec table and hard to fix after the box arrives. PETKIT's manual says to place the device on a hard, flat surface, keep it from touching the wall, and avoid tilting it, because there are weight sensors in the base. Whisker's comparison chart says the Litter-Robot 4 can be used on hard floor or carpet, with a Carpet Tray add-on required for carpet. If your only good spot is on a rug, that tray is part of the real cost of the Litter-Robot 4.

## What Reviewers Report
