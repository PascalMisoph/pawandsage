**Quick answer:** Odor control is mostly **cleaning frequency**: scoop at least daily, wash the box and replace the litter on schedule, and use an **unscented clumping litter**. Don't mask it with perfumes, sprays or essential oils, which cats may avoid and some of which are toxic to cats.

## What actually removes the smell

1. **Scoop at least once a day** (ASPCA; AAFP/ISFM-based owner guide).
2. **Wash the box and replace all litter weekly** (ASPCA), or wash every 1–4 weeks with soap and hot water (AAFP/ISFM-based guide).
3. **Use enough boxes.** One per cat plus one (Cornell). An overloaded box smells faster.
4. **Clean accidents with an enzymatic cleaner** (ASPCA). Cornell says odors must be neutralized, not just deodorized.

See our step-by-step [how to clean a litter box](/blog/how-to-clean-a-litter-box).

## The litter makes a big difference

Clumping litter lets you remove urine fully when you scoop. Most cats prefer **unscented** litter (Cornell), and many dislike aromatic or dusty litters and deodorizers (AAFP/ISFM-based guide). Compare odor-control picks in our [best cat litter guide](/blog/best-cat-litter) and the [World's Best Cat Litter review](/blog/worlds-best-cat-litter-review).

## Scents to skip

- **Scented cleaners:** the ASPCA warns cats may develop an aversion.
- **Litter deodorizers:** many cats dislike them (AAFP/ISFM-based guide).
- **Essential oil diffusers or sprays near the box:** VCA Animal Hospitals lists several essential oils as poisonous to cats; see our [tea tree oil](/blog/is-tea-tree-oil-safe-for-cats) and [peppermint oil](/blog/is-peppermint-oil-safe-for-cats) guides.
- **Covers to trap the smell:** Cornell notes covers hold odors in for the cat.

## When the smell itself is a clue

A sudden change in how urine smells, or much larger clumps, can be a medical sign. Cornell says kidney disease, thyroid disease and diabetes can increase urine volume. Mention it to your vet.

## Automatic boxes

A self-cleaning box can help with odor because waste is removed quickly, and the ASPCA says many cats accept them readily. They still need regular emptying and cleaning. See [best automatic litter box for multiple cats](/blog/best-automatic-litter-box-multiple-cats).
