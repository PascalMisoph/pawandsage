## How to Pick a Cheap Self-Cleaning Box That Lasts

We can't promise any box won't break, and we haven't run long-term tests. But you can stack the odds in your favor:

1. **Buy from a maker that publishes safety specs.** Look for named sensors (weight, infrared, motion) and a stated kitten or minimum-weight rule. PetSafe's own guide warns against unbranded rotating boxes with a single small entry and unspecified sensors.
2. **Check the warranty and return window.** CATLINK offers 1 year plus 90-day returns on direct orders, and PetSafe offers a 45-day satisfaction guarantee. Cats can take a week or more to accept a new box, so a long window matters.
3. **Match the litter rule to your budget.** Clumping boxes are cheaper to run. Crystal-tray boxes are cleaner and lower-dust but cost more each month.
4. **Measure the space.** CATLINK wants 6 inches of clearance on every side and a firm floor. Drum boxes need height, too.
5. **Keep firmware current.** Wirecutter's reporting on the original Neakasa M1 shows why safety updates matter on any connected box.

## When to Spend More Than $200

A budget box is the wrong buy if:

- **Your cat weighs more than about 22 lb.** Look at the Neakasa M1 Plus or PetSafe OpenSky (both rated to about 33 lb) in our [Litter-Robot alternatives guide](/blog/litter-robot-alternatives).
- **You have three or more cats.** Bigger drawers and better multi-cat recognition start above $200.
- **You want a sealed globe.** That's Litter-Robot territory. Our [Litter-Robot Black Friday guide](/blog/litter-robot-black-friday-deal) shows how to judge a real discount on the $699 Litter-Robot 4.

## Setting Up a Budget Box So Your Cat Uses It

Put the new box next to the old one and leave automatic cleaning off for a few days. Use the litter your cat already knows if the box allows it. If you're switching to crystal, mix it in gradually. Cornell notes that sudden litter changes can put cats off the box and that most prefer unscented litter.

Got a kitten? Keep a regular box until they meet the maker's age and weight minimum. Our [new kitten checklist](/blog/new-kitten-checklist) covers the starter setup.
