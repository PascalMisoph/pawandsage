## Really Great Cat Litter: The Brand's Best Product

The litter is what most people search for ("tuft and paw litter" is the brand's biggest product query). Tuft & Paw lists it as ultra low-tracking, 99% dust-free (triple-screened), clumping, flushable within limits and 58% lighter than clay, with a 9.25 lb bag lasting about a month for one cat. The OdorStop version adds pea litter and low-dust bentonite for multi-cat odor. Both come with a 30-day money-back guarantee under the refund policy.

The big limitation: Whisker lists Really Great Cat Litter as not recommended for Litter-Robot. For automatic boxes, use a clay from our [best litter for Litter-Robot](/blog/best-litter-for-litter-robot) guide.

## Shipping, Returns and Guarantees

From Tuft & Paw's refund policy, checked November 18, 2026:

- **U.S. shipping:** $4.95 flat rate under $49, free at $50 or more, typically 5 to 10 business days. No PO boxes.
- **Restrictions:** litter and food cannot ship outside the contiguous U.S. (not to Alaska, Hawaii or Puerto Rico).
- **Furniture returns:** within 30 days if brand new, unused and in original packaging. Used items, even lightly, cannot be returned, and return shipping is on you.
- **Litter and food:** full money-back guarantee within 30 days of purchase.

That furniture rule is the one to know: once your cat has used a box or bed, it is yours.

## Who Should Skip Tuft & Paw

- **Litter-Robot and globe-style automatic box owners** looking for litter.
- **Shoppers who want a cat tree** (none in the current catalog): see [best modern cat trees](/blog/best-modern-cat-trees).
- **Price-first buyers.** Cheaper alternatives: [pidan tofu litter](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B9MVXJNJ?tag=pawandsage21-20), the [Nature's Miracle High-Sided Corner Litter Box](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B004N1NAMW?tag=pawandsage21-20) and the [Sherpa Original Deluxe carrier](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07D5FTGP7?tag=pawandsage21-20).
