## 1. [Nature's Miracle High-Sided Corner Litter Box](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B004N1NAMW?tag=pawandsage21-20): A Bigger Box Than They Bought

Many new owners buy a small starter pan. Cat Friendly Homes recommends a litter box about **one and a half times the length of the cat**, so there's room to turn, dig and eliminate. A high-sided corner box keeps scatter in and fits neatly into a corner.

Cornell's Feline Health Center adds that most cats prefer an **uncovered box** and unscented, finer litter about one to two inches deep. An open, high-sided box ticks both boxes. For a tiny kitten, check that the entry is low enough.

## What Not to Give a New Cat Owner

- **Food or treats in bulk.** Cat Friendly Homes and the ASPCA both say to ask a vet about diet. Most new cats stay on what the shelter fed at first.
- **Scented litter or air fresheners near the box.** Cornell says most cats prefer unscented litter.
- **Loose string toys, ribbon and tinsel.** Cornell flags these as holiday hazards for cats.
- **Toxic holiday plants.** Lilies are dangerous to cats; check before gifting flowers to a cat home.
- **A surprise pet.** Never gift an animal without the new owner's say.
